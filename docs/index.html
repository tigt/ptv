Pokémon: Tabletop Version

Stories of fantastic beasts to befriend. Battles decide schoolyard bets or the fate of the world. Rediscover teambuilding, cooperation, and the mystical, scientifical, whimsical, dangerous world of Pokémon.

Pokémon: Tabletop Version is a fast, deep tabletop role-playing game that brings the mons, world, and battles you know to the imaginations of your friends. The fun you’ve had in video games, now with the adventures you’ve imagined between cutscenes.

✱ Pokémon: Tabletop Version (PTV) is fanmade and unofficial. Full disclaimer

Why play PTV?

It’s fast!
Double-blind battles: less waiting, more reacting
Most math is adding and subtracting (…and dividing by 10, but c’mon)
You already know how to play!
Played a Pokémon game? Rolled a d20? Then you already get 80% of the rules.
It’s fun!
Personalize your character and mons to be truly unique
Streamlined turns mean more tactics and interacting with the world
Mix and match what you want from the series history

Features

  • Pokémon can have a Bonus Move: a 5th slot filled by their Trainer’s Classes
  • New Abilities (many)
  • New Moves (not many)
  • Lackluster Abilities get Bonus Stats to be worth choosing
  • Pokémon gain a second Ability at level 5
  • Levels compressed to 1–20 instead of 1–100
  • Level-up movesets overhauled:
    • No dead levels without new Moves
    • Makes more Pokémon usable without guaranteed TMs (thus quicker to build)
    • Added strong Moves to weak Pokémon to power them up
    • Adds fun/fitting Moves, mythology gags, and references to Pokémon design origins
  • By default, Pokémon level up with Trainers; no tracking EXP for each
  • Trainers can be attacked, and some Trainers fight back
