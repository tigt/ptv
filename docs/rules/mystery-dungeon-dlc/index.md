# Mystery Dungeon “DLC”

- you count as both a trainer and a pokemon, rules-wise
- your features that would affect your team can affect you
- your features that would affect other pokemon in your party can affect your party's other PCs
- you can't switch, since you _are_ the Pokémon
- [in PMD lore you normally can't evolve without unlocking it first](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Evolution_(Mystery_Dungeon)), but that's up to your table
- multiple item slots?
- GMs: you might need to expand tutoring opportunities, since now players only have 5 moves at once instead of 5xpartyCount. Swapping in and out moves during rests is probably going to be much more common in this game mode
- if you find your characters don't have enough mechanical complexity, tell us and maybe we can figure something out with Rare Qualities, or more items from PMD, or… As a stopgap, you could swap out characters for each player, I guess?
- > You perform a Basic Attack, rolling 1d6 and adding your ATK modifier. This move has no Type, is unaffected by Abilities, and costs no PP.
- more specific rules for PMD-common actions (even if just for clarity)
  - throwing items: Focus for precision, Athletics for power/distance
  - checking for traps
  - identifying items?
  - detecting stairs/exits and other navigational points of interest on the current floor?
  - > Most items don’t require rolls to be used, but orbs, wands, and thrown items do. Orbs require a Focus roll to work correctly, Wands require a Finesse roll to work correctly, and thrown items require a Throw roll to work correctly.
- if you really want to run the mystery dungeons themselves specifically…
  - each room and hallway are probably their own Zone unless you want to make super huge rooms multiple zones
  - Tile-based mechanics like traps and wonder tiles will probably need to be abstracted
  - I think you reset stats and non-persistent status conditions every floor, right?
  - if you really want to get crunchy with a grid or time-tracking, this might be the wrong system for you. Have you tried [_Explorers! A PMD RPG_](TODO) or [_Pokémon Mystery Dungeon Tabletop Adventures_](TODO)?
  - > One thing you cannot do on a Mystery Dungeon’s floor is rest or take too long. If you stay on one floor for more than 30 minutes, an unseen force blows any outsiders back to the place they entered the dungeon.
- https://goblinpunch.blogspot.com/2023/04/the-underclock-fixing-random-encounter.html ?
